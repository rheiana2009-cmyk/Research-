GET TO KNOW THE PROJECT

What is SUKASUGA?

A research innovation exploring the use of coconut vinegar as an electrolyte in a small emergency-lighting prototype.

SUKASUGA is a coconut vinegar-powered emergency lamp developed to investigate whether a naturally acidic fermented liquid can help generate electrical energy when used with copper and zinc electrodes. The project evaluates its ability to power a low-voltage LED and explores its potential as an alternative lighting concept.
WHAT YOU WILL NEED

Materials and Equipment

The materials used in developing and testing the SUKASUGA innovation.

01

White LED

The white LED served as the light source of the emergency lamp. It was connected to the circuit to determine whether the electrical energy produced by the electrolyte could power the light.

02

Copper Electrode

The copper electrode served as the positive electrode (cathode) in the electrochemical cell. It worked together with the zinc electrode to help produce electrical energy.

03

Zinc Electrode

The zinc electrode served as the negative electrode (anode). It released electrons during the electrochemical reaction, contributing to the generation of electrical energy.

04

Connecting Wires

The connecting wires were used to connect the copper and zinc electrodes to the LED and multimeter. They provided a path for the electrical current to flow through the circuit.

05

Multimeter

The multimeter was used to measure the voltage produced by each electrolyte sample. The voltage readings were recorded in volts (V) for every trial.

06

Ammeter

The ammeter was used to measure the current flowing through the circuit in order to determine the electrical current produced by the device.

07

Plastic Containers

The plastic containers were used to hold the different samples separately during testing. They also helped prevent the liquids from mixing with one another.

08

Measuring Instruments

The measuring instruments were used to measure the required amounts of the samples. This helped keep the amount consistent among the treatments.

09

Coconut Vinegar

Coconut vinegar is a naturally acidic fermented liquid. In this study, it serves as the primary electrolyte base in the SUKASUGA lamp, where it facilitates electron flow between copper and zinc electrodes to generate electrical energy for emergency lighting.

THE BASIC IDEA

How It Works

The main components work together in an electrochemical cell.

Electrolyte and electrodes

The coconut vinegar acts as the electrolyte. The copper and zinc electrodes participate in an electrochemical reaction that can produce an electrical potential.

Electrical output

Connecting wires provide a path for current in the circuit. The multimeter and ammeter are used to measure electrical output, while the LED is used to check whether the prototype can provide light.
